# Code of Ethics

## Purpose

CompeteMath's high-level purpose is to be the knowledge, security, and reliability layer for agentic formal theorem provers. The lower-level purpose that this stands on is the pursuit of knowledge for the sake of God (في سبيل الله):

> "Whoever takes a path in which he seeks knowledge, Allah will make easy for him, by it, a path to Paradise."
>
> «ومن سلك طريقا يلتمس فيه علما سهل الله له به طريقا إلى الجنة»
>
> — Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, no. 2699 (narrated by Abū Hurayrah), Kitāb al-Dhikr wa-l-Duʿāʾ. Manuscript copy of 1164 CE: [Princeton University Library, Garrett MS 104Y, fol. 167a](https://dpul.princeton.edu/islamicmss/catalog/cr56n359w), [exact page](https://iiif-cloud.princeton.edu/iiif/2/4b%2F29%2F26%2F4b2926c452bf475ba690d038a6577e8b%2Fintermediate_file/full/full/0/default.jpg).

Contributors may identify, believe and live however they want. What we require is that interactions and contributions in the organisation, including any of its projects, now or later, are in line with the purpose above, as non-exhaustively elaborated below. This applies to people and to automated systems alike; whoever causes even a single line of code to run is held responsible for what it does.

## Being in line with our purpose

This looks like:

- Claiming only what you have checked, understood well enough to defend, and have had reviewed.
- Not deceiving fellow contributors and members of the organisation.
- Doing any work you take part in to the best of your ability.
- Helping and teaching, crediting others, interacting well-mannered, and leaving things better than you found them.
- Do not intentionally harm or attack any project, the people who use it, or the people working on it. Security research done for beneficial knowledge in a non disruptive manner is not considered an attack.
- Keep self-promotion, quarrels, and sharing of anything unrelated out of work.

Contributions and interactions are judged against this by their content and the intent supported by materially convincing and reviewed evidence, but never by anyone's identity, beliefs or way of life.
