This is the official GitHub CompeteMath organisation - a mirror of the research and open-source initiatives of CompeteMath. 

## 🌌 About CompeteMath

We are an independent organisation with almost no operational costs and no corporate or commercial pressure. 
This means we have a relatively rare and often overlooked luxury; We can pursue ideas deeply, explore difficult 
questions, and build infrastructure whose value might take years to materialise, without having to justify the value 
immediately. Currently, our work is targeting systems that improve the correctness, reliability, and proving performance 
of automated theorem provers, regardless of which driving LLM/Agent is used, now or in the future.

## ⚡ Our Core Philosophy

- **Independence:** CompeteMath is entirely non-commercial and politically unaligned. Our governance, contents, and
goals are strictly dedicated to the pursuit of beneficial knowledge. This is important because it empowers
unadulterated creativity.
- **Knowledge gives Power:** Machine-verified truth shouldn't be locked behind proprietary API walls or centralized labs.
We believe that accessible and world-class knowledge should be open, provided it is beneficial to the people.
- **Humanity:** We recognize that brilliant potential is too often blocked by factors outside of
reasonable control. Even if we can't solve this problem entirely, we do what we can, even if it is *just* a kind word.

## 🛠️ The Ecosystem

- **[Tengoku](https://github.com/competemath/tengoku)**: An AI-first Lean 4 formal knowledge tree. Many research libraries unified on the same toolchain.
- **[Leak](https://github.com/competemath/leak-services)**: A suite of MCP tools designed specifically for agent-driven theorem proving harnesses/pipelines.
- **[competemath.com](https://competemath.com)**: Our competitive problem-solving platform, featuring AI-generated problems with
formal Lean 4 proofs, separated from community-made problems.
