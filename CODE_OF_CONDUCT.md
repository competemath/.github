# Code of Conduct

Whatever you look like, wherever you are from, whatever you believe, and whatever you have done in the past: if you accept what
is written here and want to do well-intentioned work with us, we would be honoured for you to be part of our organisation.

## What we ask

- Keep the work focused. Self-promotion, quarrels, campaigning and anything unrelated stay out of our spaces, whatever the topic and whichever
  side of it.
- Say only what you have checked, and credit the people whose work you use.
- Be honest with the people you work with, and treat them and their projects with respect.
- Do not harass or attack anyone, or intentionally harm a project, its users or its contributors. Security research done for beneficial
  knowledge, without disruption, is not an attack.

We judge what is done here, by its content and its intent, never by who someone is or what they do elsewhere.

## Where this applies

Every CompeteMath repository, discussion, issue and pull request, and any other space where someone acts in the organisation's name.


## Exceptions to the welcome

Please do not interact with the organisation if:

- You have been banned from our spaces for earlier violations, and a request to return has been denied by an owner;
- You willingly do not accept this Code of Conduct or our [contributing](https://competemath.com/contribute) and [posting](POSTING.md) guidelines, for no valid reason communicated with a maintainer;
- You plan to break any of the policies in the individual projects of the CompeteMath ecosystem.

Nobody is turned away for anything else.

## Enforcement

- Report a concern privately to a maintainer or an owner (GitHub's "Report content", or a private message), not in a public thread.
- A first slip gets the benefit of the doubt: a reminder and a request to edit. After that, maintainers respond in proportion: removal of
  content, then a temporary or permanent block. A serious deliberate harm may skip steps.
- The person concerned is told which rule was involved and why, and can ask for one review of the decision by another maintainer or an owner.
  A maintainer who is involved in a matter does not decide it.
